AC4 neutral visual audit rubric (Stage AE human presentation)

Review only visible video evidence. Do not infer model, suite, parent, arm, dose, automatic labels, or telemetry.
Use one primary label per present clip:
STABLE_GRASP, PREMATURE_APERTURE, CONTACT_LOSS, PREMATURE_RELEASE_OR_DROP, OBJECT_DISPLACEMENT, AMBIGUOUS_OR_OCCLUDED, NOT_IDENTIFIABLE.
Use AMBIGUOUS_OR_OCCLUDED or NOT_IDENTIFIABLE when the video does not establish the event.

Stage AE is an endpoint-observability audit only. A label describes what is
visually identifiable in the clip; it does not confirm or rewrite the sealed
automatic physical endpoint.
